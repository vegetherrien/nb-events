NB Events Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026

NB Events doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, and nothing you do in the app is tracked.

What stays on your iPhone

Events you save, your reminder setting and the categories you show or hide are stored only on your device, shared between the app and its widget. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

  • The event list for the city you pick, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • The weather forecast from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), for the center of the city you're viewing (New Braunfels or Boerne), not your own location. The one exception is the nearby list described below.

Like any website, those services receive standard technical information with each request, such as your IP address. NB Events doesn't add anything that identifies you. Tapping Directions opens Apple Maps with the event's address, under Apple's own privacy policy. See GitHub's privacy statement and Open-Meteo's terms.

Your location

Location is only used when you tap Use my location, and only "while using the app." Your phone compares where you are with the cities NB Events has calendars for and switches to the closest one. Your location isn't stored, shared or sent to us.

If you're far from those cities and the nearby events list is turned on, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about a mile) to Ticketmaster to find events within 30 miles, and to Open-Meteo for the local forecast. See Ticketmaster's privacy policy.

Your home area

If you set My area, the app keeps a point for your home (from your location, a pin you drop on the map, or the middle of the neighborhood you choose) so it can show your neighborhood's events, your zoned schools, your council district and your trash day. The app downloads the same public map of zones for everyone and does the matching on your phone. Your home point and the areas it matches are stored only on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and are removed when you tap Clear my area or delete the app.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app), only when you tap Use my location, to pick a city or to set your home area.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on reminders for saved events. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Calendar (add only), only when you tap Add to Calendar. The app can add that one event but can't read your calendar.

Children

NB Events is a general-audience event calendar and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
